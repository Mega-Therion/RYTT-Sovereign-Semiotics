A compact system map

One grammar. Two planes.
No information loss.

Every Latin letter maps to a unique PUA codepoint in one of two spatially disjoint planes. Compound chords pack high-frequency sequences into single tokens. All other characters pass through unchanged.

01

Geometry as vocabulary

Twenty-six atomic glyph families and twenty-three compound chords give recurring structures a native geometric form.

02

Casing as position

Lowercase occupies the Ground Plane at Z = 0; uppercase occupies the Elevated Plane at Z = 25. The planes are disjoint by construction.

03

Proof before promise

Property-style invariant tests and reproducible Python benchmarks make reversibility inspectable rather than merely asserted.
